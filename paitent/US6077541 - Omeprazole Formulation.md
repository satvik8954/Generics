## Roles **not** explicitly stated

Following the same evidence-only methodology we've been using, I would **not** infer these:

- ❌ Cetyl alcohol → Plasticizer (not stated in the patent)
- ❌ Talc → Glidant/Lubricant (the patent explicitly calls it an **inert processing aid**)
- ❌ Lactose → Diluent (the patent uses the term **filler**)
- ❌ Sodium lauryl sulfate → Surfactant/Solubilizer (the patent uses **surface active agent**)
- ❌ Povidone → Film former (the patent uses **binder**)

This patent is actually a good example of one where almost every major excipient has its function explicitly defined, making it well suited for your evidence-only extraction pipeline.
