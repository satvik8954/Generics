# US20150196518A1 – Pharmaceutical Compositions of Mesalamine

This patent is much simpler than the previous ones. It contains **only two formulation examples**:

- **Example 1** – Percentage (% w/w) formulation
- **Example 2** – Fixed-strength 800 mg mesalamine formulation

The dosage form for both is:

> **CAPSULE** (containing an **enteric-coated caplet/tablet**)
>

# Example 1

**Dosage Form:** **CAPSULE** (containing an enteric-coated caplet)
